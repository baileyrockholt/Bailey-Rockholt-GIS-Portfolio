ArcGIS Pro • Terrain Analysis • Scientific Mapping

Graduate Thesis GIS Mapping Project

Collaborated on a graduate thesis research project by preparing spatial data and designing publication-quality maps for scientific presentation. The work required transforming raw field and elevation data into clear cartographic products suitable for a thesis and manuscript.

My Role

  • Imported and organized field sample locations from CSV files.
  • Prepared USGS elevation data in ArcGIS Pro.
  • Created study area and terrain analysis maps.
  • Designed multiple publication-ready layout options.
  • Built inset maps for regional and local context.
  • Refined labels, halos, symbology, legends, scale bars, and map hierarchy.
  • Revised maps based on researcher feedback.
  • Prepared final figures for thesis and manuscript use.
